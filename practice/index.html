Parliament and executive accountability

GS Paper II

“Parliamentary committees strengthen legislative scrutiny.” Explain the role of parliamentary committees in ensuring executive accountability. (150 words)

A useful response structure

Define the role of committees; explain scrutiny of bills, budgets, and ministry work; note limits such as recommendations being advisory; conclude with how timely reports and public accountability improve the process.
